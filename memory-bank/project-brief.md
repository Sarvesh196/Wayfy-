# Project brief

## Vision

Wayfy is a community-powered, real-time status and reliability layer for
intercity buses and trains in India. Passengers report and discuss trip
conditions; Wayfy combines recent reports into a live status. Longer term, the
product may provide live maps, reliability insights, and delay predictions.

## Users

- Intercity bus and train passengers
- People waiting to board or pick someone up
- Later: bus and train operators

## MVP scope

- Phone OTP login and user profiles
- Search and follow routes, bus numbers, and train numbers
- A room for each scheduled trip, expiring after the trip
- Group chat in trip rooms
- Quick reports: On time, Delayed, Traffic, Breakdown, Crowded, Departed,
  Arrived
- Aggregated live status with report counts
- Confirm and dispute actions on reports
- Push notifications for followed-trip status changes
- Basic user reporting and blocking

The roadmap beyond the MVP includes location and trust features, predictions
and AI assistance, and operator tools. See the product outline in the project
conversation for the detailed phase roadmap.
